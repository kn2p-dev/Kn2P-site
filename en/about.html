K&2P runs MoriPay, an all-in-one prepaid travel card that lets international visitors enjoy Korea without the hassle of exchanging money, paying or getting around.

Mission & Vision

Why we do what we do

Mission

We remove the friction international visitors to Korea face with currency exchange, payments and transport through prepaid card fintech and travel platform technology, delivering the most comfortable travel experience possible.

Vision

Through seamless digital financial connections that know no borders, we're building a travel ecosystem where people can live and spend as freely and safely in an unfamiliar city as they do at home.

Core Competencies

Our core strengths

account_balance

Robust Fintech Infrastructure

Built on partnerships with licensed, regulated financial and payment providers, including Hecto Financial (prepaid electronic payment), Shinhan Card (merchant payment network), EZL (nationwide transit) and KOMOJU (global payments), we provide secure, reliable fintech infrastructure and real-time settlement.

travel_explore

Deep Traveler Insight & UX

We study how international visitors actually travel and spend in Korea, and design an experience around it: a dedicated interface free of language barriers, with only the features travelers truly need: transit, insurance, eSIM and transfers.

Our Product

MoriPay:
Your Travel Companion

MoriPay is more than a payment app. It is a smart travel card and platform that eases the worry of exchanging money and paying in an unfamiliar country, and supports travelers at every moment of their trip.

  • check_circleEasy mobile top-up in foreign currency and an anonymous physical prepaid card
  • check_circleShinhan Card payments online and offline nationwide, plus EZL public transit
  • check_circleKorea eSIM, travel insurance and real-time balance transfers between members (transferred balances are non-refundable)
