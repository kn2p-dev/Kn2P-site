Flagship service

MoriPay: the all-in-one
prepaid travel card

MoriPay is a smart prepaid card platform that takes the hassle out of exchanging money and paying in Korea. From mobile top-up in foreign currency to payments at Shinhan Card merchants, public transit, and easy transfers between users, it all happens in one app.

  • payments

    Easy mobile top-up

    Top up a prepaid KRW balance instantly with your foreign credit card or payment network, and pay without cash.

  • contactless

    Shinhan Card merchants & EZL transit

    One physical card covers restaurants, shopping malls and convenience stores nationwide, plus the subway and city buses.

  • sync_alt

    Real-time transfers between MoriPay users

    Traveling with friends? Send KRW balance to each other in real time, with no bank transfers or settling up. *Transferred balances are non-refundable.
