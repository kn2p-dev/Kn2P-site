MoriPay

A new standard for
cashless travel in Korea

Top up in your own currency and pay freely anywhere in Korea with the physical card or your phone. Payments at millions of Shinhan Card merchants, EZL public transit and transfers between users, all on one prepaid card.

  • bolt

    Real-time FX top-up & delivery before you travel

    Top up a prepaid KRW balance instantly at the real-time exchange rate using your foreign currency (e.g. an overseas credit card). The card can be delivered to your home before you leave.

  • credit_card

    Shinhan Card merchants & instant push alerts

    The anonymous prepaid card issued by Hecto Financial supports IC chip payments at restaurants, shops and cafés across the Shinhan Card network, and notifies you in the app the moment you pay.

  • lock

    EZL transit & one-tap card lock

    Tap on for the subway and city buses. If you lose the card, lock it instantly with one tap in the app to keep your balance safe.
