Help & Support

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about using MoriPay.

Q.Where in Korea can I use the MoriPay card?expand_more
At Shinhan Card merchants. You can pay just like a regular credit card at card terminals in restaurants, cafés, malls, convenience stores, duty-free shops and traditional markets in Seoul, Busan, Jeju and other destinations.
Q.How do I top up and use the EZL transit function?expand_more
The physical card has a built-in EZL chip, a nationwide transit card standard. Top it up with Korean won cash at subway station machines or convenience stores (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven, etc.), then tap it on bus and subway readers.
Q.What exchange rate applies when I top up?expand_more
The real-time rate is shown clearly on the top-up screen in the app. Pay in foreign currency with your overseas credit card or local payment method and it is converted instantly into a prepaid KRW balance, all on your phone without visiting an exchange counter.
Q.How does the airport pickup service work?expand_more
It runs at fixed fares from Incheon International Airport (T1/T2) and Gimpo International Airport to destinations in Seoul. Set your destination in the app and prepay with your MoriPay balance; your driver will greet you at arrivals with a name board and help with large luggage. Real-time messenger support is available if your flight is delayed.
Q.Can I set up the eSIM before I leave?expand_more
Yes. Choose a plan for your travel dates in the app before departure (unlimited data, optional Korean 010 number, etc.) and a QR code is issued right away. Install the eSIM on your phone in advance and activate the line after landing in Korea to start using 5G data.
Q.Is my balance protected if I lose my card?expand_more
As soon as you notice, turn on "Pause card" in the card menu of the MoriPay app and offline payments are blocked immediately. Your prepaid balance stays safely in your app account.
Q.Can I get a refund on balance I sent to another MoriPay user?expand_more
No. Real-time balance transfers between MoriPay users are credited to the recipient immediately, and transferred balances are non-refundable. Please double-check the recipient and amount before sending.
